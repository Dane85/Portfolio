Earning a black belt in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a grueling, 10-to-15 year crucible. Under Justin Bruckmann — one of Canada’s most respected pioneer martial artists and former UFC competitor — nothing was given; every stripe and belt was forged through thousands of rounds of live sparring against elite practitioners.

Jiu-jitsu is kinetic problem-solving under maximum physical pressure. It teaches you that panic is useless, that precision beats brute force, and that no matter how complex the bind, there is always an angle, a leverage point, and a path forward.

Composure Under Pressure

When a 220lb heavyweight is crushing your chest, panic guarantees defeat. You learn to breathe, analyze your micro-frames, and calmly work out of the tightest spots. That same composure applies when production systems go down.

Leverage & Precision

Helio Gracie designed BJJ so that a smaller, technically sound practitioner can overcome raw strength. In software architecture and life, finding the fulcrum and applying smart leverage solves problems brute force never can.

Perpetual White Belt Mindset

A black belt is just a white belt who never quit. Even with 15+ years on the mats, there is always someone who can catch you and always a new detail to master. Ego has no place on the mats or in engineering.

📚 06 / Reading Vault

Books I’ve Read & Recommend.

A curated shelf of titles that have influenced my thinking across computer science, engineering, space, and mental models. We will expand this as we go!

Systems Architecture

Designing Data-Intensive Applications

by Martin Kleppmann

The gold standard for understanding distributed data systems, reliability, replication, partitioning, transactions, and consensus protocols.

Key takeaway: Reliable architectures under network failure.
Aerospace & SpaceX

Liftoff: Elon Musk and the Desperate Early Days

by Eric Berger

The exhilarating account of Falcon 1's three failures on Kwajalein Atoll and the ragtag engineering crew that saved SpaceX from bankruptcy on launch 4.

Key takeaway: Radical persistence when the stakes are existential.
Software Craftsmanship

The Pragmatic Programmer

by David Thomas & Andrew Hunt

Essential philosophy on DRY code, orthogonal systems, taking responsibility for your architecture, and being a continuous learner.

Key takeaway: Software is a craft of continuous refinement.
Mindset & Habits

Atomic Habits

by James Clear

How tiny 1% compound improvements accumulate into transformative mastery over years — the exact principle behind earning a BJJ black belt and completing a computer science degree.

Key takeaway: Systems beat goals every single time.
Stoic Philosophy

Meditations

by Marcus Aurelius

Timeless reflections on internal discipline, focusing strictly on what is in your control, and maintaining emotional equilibrium in high-friction environments.

Key takeaway: The obstacle is the way.
Sci-Fi Epic

Dune

by Frank Herbert

A masterpiece exploring ecology, human potential, prescience, and political intrigue on Arrakis. A foundational work of speculative imagination.

Key takeaway: Fear is the mind-killer.
