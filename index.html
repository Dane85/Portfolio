Calm under pressure

When someone heavy is crushing your frames, panic loses. You breathe and work the escape. That calm carries over when production systems misbehave.

Jiu-jitsu is built on finding the fulcrum. In engineering too, the right point of leverage solves what raw effort can't.

05 · Community

Showing up

Durham Region, July 2019.

When tragedy hits your community, standing on the sidelines isn't an option.

In July 2019, my friend Nichole McWatters of Oshawa lost her life at Frenchman's Bay in Pickering, after swimming out to save her six-year-old son, who was carried safely to shore. I organized a community fund to support her son's care and education. Durham Region rallied behind it, and it raised more than $18,000.
